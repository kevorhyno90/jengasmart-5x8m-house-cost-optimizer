Structural & Roof

Mono-Pitch (Skillion) vs Traditional Gable Roof

Mono-pitch slopes in one direction (e.g. front to back). Eliminates ridge caps, valley gutters, and saves 30% on timber trusses and 4 iron sheets.

Saves approx: KES 28,500 High Impact
Procurement Logistics

Buy Sand & Ballast Direct from Source in 14-Tonne Tipper

Ordering a single large 14-tonne tipper directly from the river/quarry instead of multiple 7-tonne or pickup trips from roadside middlemen saves substantial broker markups.

Saves approx: KES 14,000 Easy Win
Labor Management

Milestone-Based Labor ("Kandarasi") vs Daily Wages

Daily wages (Fundi @ KES 1,500/day + Helper @ KES 800/day) leads to slow work and budget drag. Fixed milestone tasks with 10% retention guarantee fast delivery and zero day-stretching.

Saves approx: KES 25,000 Critical Protection
Materials Selection

Dual Cement Grade (32.5N for Plaster vs 42.5N for Concrete)

Don't use expensive 42.5N structural cement for wall mortar and wall plaster. Use standard 32.5N (KES ~750/bag vs KES 850/bag) for the 50 bags of mortar/plaster.

Saves approx: KES 6,500 Smart Practice
Move-In Acceleration

Phase 1 Move-In: Red Oxide Cement Screed (Defer Floor Tiles)

Polished red/black oxide cement floor screed costs KES 12,000 vs Ceramic tiles with adhesive & grouting (KES 55,000+). Allows safe, dust-free move-in; tiles can be laid later directly on top.

Saves approx: KES 43,000 Huge Cashflow Relief
Roof Framing

Engineered Truss Spacing (1.2m c/c with 2×2 Purlins)

For light gauge-28 mabati, 1.2m center-to-center truss spacing is structurally sound and reduces 3 full truss assemblies compared to overly conservative 0.9m spacing.

Saves approx: KES 16,000 Structural Saving
