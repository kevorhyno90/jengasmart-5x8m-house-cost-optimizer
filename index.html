Structural & Roof

Mono-Pitch (Skillion) vs Traditional Gable Roof

Mono-pitch slopes in one direction (e.g. front to back). Eliminates ridge caps, valley gutters, and saves 30% on timber trusses and 4 iron sheets.

Saves approx: KES 28,500 High Impact
Procurement Logistics

Buy Sand & Ballast Direct from Source in 14-Tonne Tipper

Ordering a single large 14-tonne tipper directly from the river/quarry instead of multiple 7-tonne or pickup trips from roadside middlemen saves substantial broker markups.

Saves approx: KES 14,000 Easy Win
Labor Management

Milestone-Based Labor ("Kandarasi") vs Daily Wages

Daily wages (Fundi @ KES 1,500/day + Helper @ KES 800/day) leads to slow work and budget drag. Fixed milestone tasks with 10% retention guarantee fast delivery and zero day-stretching.

Saves approx: KES 25,000 Critical Protection
Materials Selection

Dual Cement Grade (32.5N for Plaster vs 42.5N for Concrete)

Don't use expensive 42.5N structural cement for wall mortar and wall plaster. Use standard 32.5N (KES ~750/bag vs KES 850/bag) for the 50 bags of mortar/plaster.

Saves approx: KES 6,500 Smart Practice
Move-In Acceleration

Phase 1 Move-In: Red Oxide Cement Screed (Defer Floor Tiles)

Polished red/black oxide cement floor screed costs KES 12,000 vs Ceramic tiles with adhesive & grouting (KES 55,000+). Allows safe, dust-free move-in; tiles can be laid later directly on top.

Saves approx: KES 43,000 Huge Cashflow Relief
Roof Framing

Engineered Truss Spacing (1.2m c/c with 2×2 Purlins)

For light gauge-28 mabati, 1.2m center-to-center truss spacing is structurally sound and reduces 3 full truss assemblies compared to overly conservative 0.9m spacing.

Saves approx: KES 16,000 Structural Saving
Raw Materials Economy

Replace / Blend River Sand with Quarry Dust (Vumbi ya Mawe)

Quarry dust costs KES 800/ton vs River Sand at KES 1,600/ton. Blending 50% quarry dust + 50% river sand for blinding, slab & brick mortar slashes raw aggregate costs without compromising structural integrity.

Saves approx: KES 18,500 High Value Replacement

The 4 Non-Negotiable Fundi Rules for Devin's 2-Meter Extension

Strict structural guidelines for your Sotik mason to ensure the new extension NEVER cracks or separates

1
Drill & Rebar-Stitch the Floor Slab

Drill 12mm holes 150mm deep into the front face of the existing 5×6m slab every 300mm. Blow out stone dust, wet with cement slurry, and hammer in 400mm Y10 rebar dowels before pouring the new 10m² slab.

⚠️ Rule: Never cast the new slab as a loose butt joint! Without rebar dowels, the floor will develop a crack right across the living room within 6 months.
2
Toothing the Existing Brick Walls

Before laying the new 2.0m side walls, have the fundi chip out alternating brick pockets ("toothing") on the front corners of the existing brick walls. The new bricks must interlock directly into the old wall.

⚠️ Rule: Add hoop iron ties (25mm gauge) in mortar beds every 3 courses between the old brick wall and new brickwork.
3
Cast a Monolithic Perimeter Ring Beam

Do NOT build separate ring beams! Form and cast ONE continuous 200×200mm reinforced concrete ring beam (with 4× D8 rebar and R6 stirrups @ 200mm) running around ALL 4 walls: the old 6m walls, the new 2m extensions, and the new front wall.

⭐ Benefit: The continuous ring beam clamps old and new walls together into a single rigid structural box.
4
Mono-Pitch Rake & Roof Anchor

Rake the side walls from 3.60m at the new front wall down to 2.85m at the existing brick back wall (10° fall). Cast hoop iron straps into the concrete ring beam every 800mm to anchor the 4×2 cypress rafters against strong Rift Valley winds.

🌧️ Drainage: 100% of rainwater flows toward the existing back wall into a single 5m gutter feeding your water tank.

Buy direct from local kiln operators along river banks at KES 6.50 – 7.50 per brick. Avoid broker markup at town hardware yards.

River Sand

Coordinate with tipper drivers offloading near Sotik market or Kaplong junction. A 10-tonne lorry costs KES 12,000 – 13,500 delivered to site.

Machine Ballast

Cypress Timber

High density, straight-grain 4×2 rafters at KES 36 – 40/ft and 2×2 purlins at KES 18 – 22/ft. Much cheaper than Nairobi yards (KES 65/ft).
